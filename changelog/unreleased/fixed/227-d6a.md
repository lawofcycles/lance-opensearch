- A `search_after` cursor whose first sort clause is `_score` (`sort: [{"_score": "desc"}, {"id": "asc"}]`, `search_after: [1.0, 100]`) no longer fails with 500 `class_cast_exception`: the executor types the cursor against its Lucene sort through `SearchAfterBuilder.buildFieldDoc`, as the shard path does, and a cursor of the wrong length answers the shard path's 400 message. A cursor without a Lucene sort (`sort` absent, or a lone descending `_score`) answers 400 `Sort must contain at least one field.` instead of being ignored. (#224)
- The `_source` element of a search body (`_source: false`, an includes list, an includes / excludes object) is applied on the fragment path; before, every hit carried the full synthesised source whatever the body asked. (#225)
