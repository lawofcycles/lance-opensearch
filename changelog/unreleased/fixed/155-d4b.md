- A query naming the derived tokens column of a `text_analyzer` override (`<column>__lance_tokens`, or the declared `derived_column_name`) no longer counts `hits.total` through Lance metadata while the hits come back empty: the derived column is a Lance column the OpenSearch mapping does not expose, so the planner now keeps such a query on the Lucene side like an `ip` or `geo_point` predicate, and `hits.total` and the hits agree (both empty).
