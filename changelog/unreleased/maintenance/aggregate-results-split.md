- `LanceAggregateResults` is split by responsibility into four package private collaborators under `execute/`: `AggregateSpecResolver` (pairs the request's aggregation builders with the pushed aggregate and produces a `ResolvedAggregate` of levels, composite sources, metrics and the top-k selection), `AggregateScanRunner` (the Lance scans: the fragment group fan out, the percentiles bin rounds, the row loops and the merge of partials), `GroupAggregationState` (the columnar group table, metric columns, bounded top-k selection and the boxed group states the scans fill), and `AggregationResultAssembler` (turns the merged state into the `InternalAggregation` objects per kind). The shared Arrow scalar readers move to `ArrowRowValues`. `LanceAggregateResults` keeps `resolve`, `execute`, `Result`, the settings hooks and the wiring, and shrinks from about 3,800 lines to under 300. Method bodies moved unchanged; queries, settings, the wire format and every existing test are untouched.
