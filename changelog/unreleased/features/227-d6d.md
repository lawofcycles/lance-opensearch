- Every aggregation type runs on the fragment executors; the coordinator no longer routes a request to the shard path because of its aggregation tree. The types the former allow list excluded are served through the stock aggregators over the fragment leaves and answer what the shard path answered: `multi_terms`, `matrix_stats`, `weighted_avg`, `median_absolute_deviation`, `variable_width_histogram` (approximate across executors, as across shards), `adjacency_matrix`, `sampler` and `diversified_sampler` (each collection slice of each executor keeps `shard_size` documents, as a slice does under concurrent segment search; `lance.fragment_path.slices: 1` gives `shard_size` per executor), `nested` and `reverse_nested` (the fragment leaves carry the parent join the aggregators read), the geo aggregations over a `geo_point` override column (`geo_distance`, `geohash_grid`, `geotile_grid`, `geo_centroid`, `geo_bounds`), scripted builders (`scripted_metric`, a `script` on a field aggregation or a `composite` source, a `script` query in a `filter` bucket; the executors run them through the node's script service as they run script queries), and `filter` / `filters` buckets over any query, a Lance full text or `lance_knn` clause included (one Lance scan per bucket on each executor, as on the shard path). The pushdown into the Lance scan is unchanged; the new shapes take the aggregators. (#227)
