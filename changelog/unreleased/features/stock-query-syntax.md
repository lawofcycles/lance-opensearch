- OpenSearch's stock full text syntax runs on the Lance inverted index the way the `lance_*` queries do. The coordinator rewrites a `match`, `match_phrase` or `multi_match` (type `best_fields`) on a `lance_text` field into the `lance_match`, `lance_match_phrase` or `lance_multi_match` clause with the same parameters before it plans a request, per target index from its mapping, and ships the rewritten query to the executors; `GET /<index>/_lance/explain` applies the same rewrite. `operator`, `prefix_length`, `max_expansions`, `slop`, per field boosts (`title^2`), `boost` and `_name` now reach Lance, `fuzziness` becomes the edit distance (`AUTO` resolved on the whole query text) instead of a 400, and word order and `slop` are enforced on `match_phrase`. A stock `bool` whose `must` and `should` lists hold full text clauses fuses them into one `lance_fts_bool` under the same occurrences (full text clauses in `must_not` join it, scalar `filter` / `must_not` clauses become the scan's SQL prefilter), so `bool { must: [match, match], filter: [range] }` plans as one prefiltered Lance scan with Lance summing the clause scores as Lucene's `BooleanQuery` would; a `should` next to a `filter` without a `must`, a `minimum_should_match`, a boost on the `bool`, or a boosted `match_phrase` inside the fused bool keeps the bool on the Lucene side with unchanged results. A `match_phrase` outside the rewrite's reach (inside a `function_score`, a `nested`, ...) now answers Lance's phrase query through the field type as well. A clause boost inside `lance_fts_bool` / `lance_fts_boost` now scales the Lance match or multi match it sits on instead of being dropped; on a phrase or nested boolean clause it answers 400, since Lance carries no factor there. The `lance_*` queries stay as the explicit form; the docs present the stock syntax first.
