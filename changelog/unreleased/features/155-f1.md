- The planner carries two request demandable traits next to the convention of every operator, `Accuracy` (`EXACT`, or `APPROXIMATE` for an aggregate with a `cardinality`, `percentiles` or `percentile_ranks` metric, which the pushed Lance scan and the Lucene aggregators compute as the same sketches) and `TieStability` (`STABLE_ROWADDR` for a bare scan or an unsorted scalar page, `STABLE_KEY` for a page ordered by a stored column on the pushed scan or on the heap collector, `UNSTABLE` for a page in score order out of a full text or knn scan). Every physical operator declares its values from the tree it stands in for, the handoff, fan out and merge inherit their input's, and `RequestPlanner` derives what the request demands: an explicit `track_total_hits` (an integer or `true`) demands an exact count, a `search_after` cursor over a planned page demands a reproducible tie order. The Volcano run is asked to meet the demand, so a costlier plan that declares the demanded value wins over a cheaper one that does not, and a request no plan can satisfy is refused with a 400 whose message names the trait and what the plan offers (`plan_failed: ... track_total_hits requires Accuracy [exact] but every plan offers Accuracy [approximate] ...`). Two shapes change observably: `track_total_hits` with an explicit bound next to a `cardinality` or `percentiles` aggregation is refused instead of answering a sketch under an exact count, and `search_after` over a `lance_match` page sorted by `_score` alone is refused at plan time on the coordinator (with the trait message) instead of on the executor. Every other plan is unchanged; `GET /<index>/_lance/explain` answers the same refusals for the same bodies and does not print the traits yet. (#155)
