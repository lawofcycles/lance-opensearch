- `min_score`, `terminate_after`, `stored_fields`, `docvalue_fields`, `fields`, `"explain": true` and a score order `search_after` cursor are served on the fragment executors instead of routing the request to the shard path. The executor composes the stock `MinimumScoreCollector` and an early terminating counter around its hits, count and aggregation collectors the way the shard path's query phase does, so `hits.total` under either knob is what the collectors saw (for every query shape, a pushed full text query included) and `terminated_early` is reported when any executor stopped at the bound; hits are rendered through the stock fetch sub phases (`FetchSourcePhase`, `FetchDocValuesPhase`, `FetchFieldsPhase`, `ExplainPhase`) over the fragment readers, so `_source` filtering, the projections and `_explanation` answer as on the shard path. A `_explanation` of a full text or `lance_knn` hit now carries the hit's score from the Lance scan instead of `0.0`. `terminated_early` is left out of a response whose body has no `terminate_after` (it was always `false` before). The `ShardPathReason` constants `MIN_SCORE`, `TERMINATE_AFTER`, `STORED_FIELDS`, `DOCVALUE_FIELDS`, `EXPLAIN_PER_HIT` and `SEARCH_AFTER_SCORE` are gone. (#227)
