- The score shaping compounds of the query DSL translate to the rows they match where no score is read. A `constant_score`, `dis_max`, `boosting` or `function_score` at the top of a count, an aggregation request or a page ordered by stored columns now becomes the predicate of its filter, the union of its clauses, its positive clause or its query, so the request runs as a Lance count scan, a pushed aggregate or a pushed sorted page instead of Lucene's collectors over doc values queries; a `function_score` with `min_score` or a `script_score` function, and the `script_score` query, keep the Lucene composition under every envelope, where the script runs and fails. On a request that reads scores (a page in score order, a sort naming `_score`, `track_scores`, `min_score`, `rescore`, `collapse`) the four compounds keep the Lucene composition, which orders the page the way the query asks, and `GET /<index>/_lance/explain` names the choice (`query type [dis_max] on a scored request`). A `bool` whose optional `should` clauses would score its matches apart (a `should` next to a `must` or `filter`, or several `should`) now takes the Lucene composition on such a request as well, so the page order agrees with an ordinary index where the pushed page used to report every hit at `1.0` in row address order; under a count, an aggregation or a column sort it translates as before. The field pre-flight for `ip` and `geo_point` override columns descends into the compounds. `docs/features.md` gains a query DSL matrix naming, for every stock query type, whether the Lance scan, the Lance inverted index or the Lucene composition over the fragment leaves evaluates it, or whether it is refused or not served; `LanceQueryDSLIT` runs every row under four envelopes on the fragment path and the stock search action and compares the answers.
