- `rescore` and `collapse` are served on the fragment executors instead of routing the request to the shard path. Each executor collects the rescorers' largest `window_size` through the Lucene collector, runs the stock `QueryRescorer` over it under `query_weight`, `rescore_query_weight` and `score_mode`, and returns its `from + size` page; the coordinator merges the pages by the new scores, so with several data nodes each executor rescores its own window as each shard does on a multi shard index. A `collapse` request collects one hit per distinct value through the stock `CollapsingTopDocsCollector` over the field's doc values (`keyword` or a number, as on the shard path), the coordinator keeps one hit per value across the executors, renders the collapse value under `fields`, and expands `inner_hits` with one multi search of ordinary fragment path searches bounded by `max_concurrent_group_searches`. Neither shape is planned as a pushed page. The refusals follow the shard path with its messages, answered as 400: `rescore` with a `sort`, a `window_size` above `index.max_rescore_window`, `collapse` on a field that is not a keyword or number with doc values, `collapse` with `rescore`, `collapse` with `search_after` under a sort other than the collapse field, and `collapse` in a scroll context. `GET /{index}/_lance/explain` reports both shapes on the fragment route with `unplanned` set to `rescore or collapse (a second pass over the Lucene collector's page)`. The `ShardPathReason` constants `COLLAPSE` and `RESCORE` are gone. (#227)
